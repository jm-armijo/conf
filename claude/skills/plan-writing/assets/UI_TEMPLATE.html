Retry policy for the payments gateway client

Make transient gateway failures invisible to callers by retrying idempotent requests with jittered backoff, while guaranteeing a charge is never submitted twice.

Scope 5 files Risk Medium Blocked on R2

Problem

  • Terminal failures: GatewayClient treats all non-2xx responses as fatal errors immediately.
  • Customer impact: Valid cards are declined with "Payment failed" during the provider's nightly 5-second failover window.
  • Operational friction: Requires manual dead-letter queue replays; failures are discovered by customers before monitoring triggers.

Approach

  • Isolate logic: Extract retry orchestration into a separate RetryPolicy object wrapping the transport.
  • Stateless decisions: Policy owns only the mathematical backoff curve and knows nothing of payments or HTTP.
  • Idempotency guarantee: Mint an Idempotency-Key once per logical operation to ensure safety on ambiguous timeouts.
  • Two tasks: The policy object and its curve ship first with no caller changes; wiring it into GatewayClient follows, so a reviewer can judge the backoff maths without the transport diff on top of it.

Components

RetryPolicy new

  • Role: Pure function answering "retry after how long?".
  • Algorithm: Exponential base 2 from 100ms, capped at 2s, with full jitter.
  • Limits: Hard ceiling of 4 attempts.

Must not know: HTTP status codes, payments, the clock, or how to execute a sleep thread.

GatewayClient edit

  • Role: Executes the attempt loop and emits retry.attempt metrics.
  • State: Re-sends the exact same request object and idempotency key on failure.

Must not know: The backoff curve or max attempt count (injected via policy).

Risks & open questions

  1. Retry amplification during a real outage. Four attempts per checkout quadruples load on a struggling service.

    Mitigation: The 4-attempt ceiling plus full jitter bounds it at 4× with the peak spread over ~3.9s.

  2. Is a timeout retryable? A read timeout is ambiguous; the charge may have settled.

    Decision needed: Confirm if the provider registers keys on receipt or completion. Blocks task 2.
